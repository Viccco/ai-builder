Production day

1. Sampling policy

60 exchanges went out today. You can read 12. Decide how many go to a random slice and which conditions claim the rest, in priority order.
Random is the only part of the sample that can find something nobody thought to look for. Targeted is the only part that reliably finds what you already fear. A policy with none of the first goes blind, and a policy with none of the second wastes the budget.

2. Guardrails

Each rule matches text in the reply and carries a decision. Block means it never goes out. Route means a human sends it instead. Flag means it goes out and lands at the top of your review queue.
Watch the match counts. A rule that fires on a third of traffic is not a guardrail, it is a redesign of the product.
