Judge workbench
What you are building here, and the steps

The goal. In module 1 you read 30 drafts and decided pass or fail yourself. That does not scale, because next week there are 3000. So you write a prompt that makes a model grade the way you graded, then you measure how often it agrees with you. A judge nobody has checked is just a second opinion with no track record.

One judge, one failure mode. You are not building an "is this reply good" grader. You build one for a single mode from your own taxonomy. A reply can be perfectly grounded and tonally offensive, and a judge forced to hold both questions at once produces errors that look random.

The hard part is the boundary, not the obvious cases. Anyone can see that inventing a delivery time is a failure. Your judge earns its keep on the near misses, so your definitions have to separate the failure you pinned from the pass that sits closest to it.

  1. Pick the mode this judge is for.
  2. Calibrate it. Go through the traces and say, for each one, whether it fails this mode. Your module 1 verdict answered a different question, so it cannot be the answer key here.
  3. Write the four boxes and add two or three examples from your own labels.
  4. Copy the prompt for Claude Code, which runs it and writes the results back into this page. Reload and the scoreboard fills in.
  5. Work the disagreements, decide what each one means, then amend and rerun. Two or three rounds is normal.
